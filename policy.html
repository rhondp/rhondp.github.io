Introduction

This Privacy Policy details the commitment of umeex ("we", "our", or "us") to protecting the privacy of players and users of our mobile games and applications published on the Google Play Store. We believe in transparency and keeping your gaming experience simple, safe, and private.

No Data Collection or Storage

We take your privacy seriously. Our policy regarding your personal data is straightforward:

  • We do not collect any personal data, contact information, or device identifiers.
  • We do not store any details about you or your usage habits on any external servers.
  • We do not track your location, behavior, or gameplay interactions.
  • No account registration is required to play our games.

Offline Playability

Our games are fully functional and playable offline. Since the core gameplay does not require an active internet connection, no data is transmitted to us or any third parties during offline play. Any game progress (such as unlocked levels or local high scores) is stored safely and locally on your device. You can erase this data at any time by clearing the application's cache or uninstalling the game from your device.

Third-Party Services

To enhance your gameplay experience and support our development, our games may integrate standard third-party services provided exclusively by Google LLC:

  • Google Play Services: Enables features such as player authentication, achievements, leaderboards, and cloud-saved game progress.
  • Google AdMob: Serves advertisements within the game to help fund our indie game development.

Please note that only Google has access to the identifiers and data processed by these integrations. We do not have access to, control over, or storage of any information collected by Google.
